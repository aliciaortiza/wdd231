Crafting the Digital Identity of Humo y Sabor

A professional planning and design strategy document outlining the upcoming retail, wholesale, and storytelling website for an artisanal smoked food family business.

01

Project Information

Site Name

Humo y Sabor

Reason for Site Name

"Humo y Sabor" represents the essence of the business. The products are traditionally smoked using artisanal methods, and flavor is the primary value delivered to customers. The name is memorable, authentic, and communicates both the smoking process and the premium quality of the products.

Site Purpose

Humo y Sabor is a family-owned smoked food business. The future website will promote and sell smoked products such as Colombian sausages, smoked beef, smoked chicken, ribs, and future smoked specialties. The website will:

  • Promote the business and display products.
  • Share the family story and rich heritage.
  • Provide contact information and show business location.
  • Support online ordering and wholesale inquiries.
  • Support retail customers and increase brand visibility to help grow the business.

Project Background

This project is inspired by a real family business. My mother has dreamed of building this business for many years and is now turning that dream into reality. As a software development student, I want to support her by creating a professional website that helps promote the business, reach more customers, and strengthen the brand.

02

Target Audience & Scenarios

Primary Audience

Our segment target profile spans domestic and business clients alike:

  • Families: Looking for nutritious, easy-to-prepare, premium smoked proteins for dynamic meals.
  • Food Enthusiasts: Seeking unique artisanal flavors, traditional smoked techniques, and Colombian-inspired tastes.
  • Local Customers: Community members who support local, family-owned gourmet culinary startups.
  • B2B Buyers (Restaurants, Grocery stores, Distributors): Professional culinary buyers searching for authentic, high-volume smoked meats of consistent gourmet quality.

User Scenarios & Solutions

1. Where can I purchase smoked products online and what products are available?

Solution: The future website will host a dedicated "Products" page featuring a dynamic catalog of our smoked products. Each item card will include clear pricing, description, and an "Add to Cart" or "Order Now" feature linked to local shopping preferences.

2. How can I place a wholesale order for my restaurant or business?

Solution: A robust Wholesale Form will be embedded within the Contact page. It will allow business entities to input their business name, volume requirements, product interest, and scheduled frequency to generate a structured B2B inquiry directly to the administration.

3. What makes Humo y Sabor different from other smoked food businesses?

Solution: The Home page will showcase a beautifully styled "Our Story" section outlining the brand's Colombian roots, artisanal smoking processes, and family dedication. Key visual badges will highlight "100% Artisanal" and "Traditional Wood Smoked".

4. Where is the business located and how can I contact the owners?

Solution: The footer will contain instant contact details, while the "Contact" page will feature an embedded responsive map displaying our precise physical location, complete with interactive operating hours, telephone, email, and social media channels.

Color Scheme

The palette represents a premium, gourmet dark aesthetic utilizing rich smoked tones, clean cream backgrounds, sophisticated gold accents, and subtle, meaningful nods to Colombian roots.

#121212

Premium Black

Purpose: Primary background and main text contrast canvas.

Future Use: Backgrounds, navigation headers, structural frames, and solid contrast elements.

Accessibility: Serves as the high-contrast backdrop for the Light Cream and Premium Gold typography, securing a contrast ratio well above the AAA threshold.

#5C2E1F

Smoked Wood Brown

Purpose: Structural accents, secondary titles, and container borders.

Future Use: Card backdrops, hover borders, button outline actions, and decorative smoke overlays.

Accessibility: Combined exclusively with highly contrasted text (such as Warm and Light Cream) to keep reading simple, legible, and visual.

#D4A017

Premium Gold

Purpose: Highlights, key call-to-action details, borders, and main labels.

Future Use: Price tags, interactive icons, active link statuses, primary buttons, and title borders.

Accessibility: High visibility when used over dark elements. Ensures emphasis on elements without losing user visual tracking.

#F9EAC8

Warm Cream

Purpose: Main typography color and primary readable element surfaces.

Future Use: Paragraphs, list elements, cards, description sections, and navigation links.

Accessibility: Soft to the eyes while maintaining high legibility over #121212 and #5C2E1F, preventing eye strain.

#FDF8ED

Light Cream

Purpose: Highlights inside dark areas, content backgrounds in bright elements.

Future Use: Active focus elements, modal content backdrops, form text fields background.

Accessibility: Incredible contrast when rendering deep text. Easily reads against very dark backgrounds or holds black text with flawless clarity.

#CA0F25

Colombian Red

Purpose: Strategic highlight accents evoking national roots and smoky heat.

Future Use: Promotional tags, special culinary badges, spicy flavor level indicators.

Accessibility: Kept as an accent indicator only. Never used for dense paragraphs to prevent color-blindness reading blocks.

#003893

Colombian Blue

Purpose: Subtle visual branding anchor representing traditional security and heritage.

Future Use: Trust icons, dynamic shipping confirmations, and selective secondary visual markers.

Accessibility: Paired strictly with white/cream overlays. Kept to localized decorations to avoid distracting primary flows.

#FCD116

Colombian Yellow

Purpose: Vibrant brand highlight color conveying warmth, brightness, and energy.

Future Use: Flash sales, special product alerts, rating star icons, and highlight badges.

Accessibility: Only rendered against deep black (#121212) or dark wood brown backdrops, keeping high contrast standards.

Future Site Architecture

A balanced sitemap planned to optimize consumer conversion, showcase family business storytelling, and secure high B2B lead generation.

Page 1

Home (index.html)

Purpose: Introduce the brand, hook first-time visitors, and summarize key offerings.

Features dynamic highlights of our best-selling artisanal meats, quick-link navigation paths, an immersive "Our Story" family background teaser, customer testimonials, and a newsletter sign-up container.

Page 2

Products (products.html)

Purpose: Present the full smoked catalog in an interactive, filterable interface.

Purpose: Facilitate retail support and capture heavy volume B2B leads.

Features a dual contact layout. One section accommodates standard customer inquiries, location details, and active operating hours, while the other features a detailed Wholesale Quote request form.

Future Functionality Plan

How the completed Humo y Sabor platform will satisfy all core and advanced technical requirements for WDD 231 using professional, modern vanilla development.

Core Technical

Data Fetching & Async Workflow

All catalog details will be managed in an independent, local JSON file. During client navigation, a JavaScript fetch operation utilizes structured async/await encapsulated inside clean try/catch error blocks. This guarantees a fast, modern catalog loading screen and solid offline fault-tolerance.

Dynamic UI

Dynamic Content Render (15+ Items)

A minimum of 15 unique, high-quality smoked products will populate the dynamic JSON structure. Handled properties include: Product Name, Category, Price, Description, and Image. Each visual card will be rendered programmatically in real-time, matching screen constraints without hardcoded HTML duplications.

ES6 Standards

Array Processing & Template Literals

The code architecture uses native ES6 features. Native map(), filter(), and forEach() array methods will organize real-time search queries and category filters. Custom cards will render cleanly through high-performance ES6 Template Literals, injecting accurate markup cleanly into targeted DOM hooks.

Modularity

ES Modules & Clean Architecture

JavaScript codebases will use structured ES modules to ensure clean code architecture. Responsibilities are split cleanly into multiple files, such as dynamic data files, storage functions, and rendering blocks. This keeps script development highly organized, scalable, and easy to maintain.

Local Storage

User Preferences / Favorite Storage

A Favorite Products selection or standard Shopping Preference dashboard will connect directly with Local Storage. Users can toggle preferences, saving choices that persist across sessions, allowing returning users to quickly view their favorite smoked items.

DOM & Modals

Modal Dialog Detail Integration

To keep the interface clean, clicking "Quick View" on a product card will open a responsive modal dialog containing detailed specifications and ingredients. The modal supports full keyboard interactions and dynamic close triggers to ensure built-in accessibility.
