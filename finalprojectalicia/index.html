Our Heritage

Crafted by Hand, Guided by Ancestral Smoke

Humo y Sabor began in our home kitchen, rooted in the memories of Colombian valleys where woodsmoke signified community. Craving the rich, spiced, slow-cooked profiles of authentic Colombian recipes, our family designed custom smokehouses using premium native hardwoods.

What began as a passion for recreating our heritage soon turned into a small gourmet enterprise. Today, we supply discerning families and premium kitchens who understand that true flavor cannot be mass-produced. Every sausage linked, chicken turned, and rib-rack smoked honors that original commitment to slow, honest, handcrafted food.
