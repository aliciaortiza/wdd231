Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your smoked products fully cooked?

Yes. All our products are completely hot-smoked to safe internal food temperatures inside our modern smokehouses, ensuring they are fully cooked and ready to slice or quickly heat and serve.

How long do the products remain fresh?

Because we smoke products without artificial chemical preservatives, vacuum-sealed items will stay fresh for up to 14 days in the refrigerator. Once opened, we suggest consuming them within 5 days.

Do you offer vacuum-sealed shipping?

Yes. All orders are packed using premium commercial vacuum-sealing machines and placed in insulated containers with cold packs to preserve deep freshness during standard short transit times.

Are your seasoning glazes gluten-free?

Our Smoked Chorizo, Ribs, and Fish are free from gluten.
