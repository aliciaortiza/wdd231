Project Branding

Site Name

Humo y Sabor

Reasoning & Meaning

The name represents the essence of the business. The products are traditionally smoked using artisanal techniques, and flavor is the main value offered to customers. The name is simple, memorable, and communicates both the preparation process and the quality of the products. "Humo" evokes the aroma of slow-burning premium wood, and "Sabor" guarantees a superior, authentic culinary outcome.

Business Objectives

Site Purpose

Humo y Sabor is a website for a family-owned smoked food business. The website will promote and sell smoked products such as Colombian sausages, smoked beef, smoked chicken, ribs, and future smoked specialties.

It will provide clear information about the company, showcase products with rich visual styling, support direct online orders, provide business contact details, display physical business locations, and handle wholesale inquiries from restaurants, stores, and distributors alongside standard retail purchases from individual consumers.

User Scenarios

Target Audience & Use Cases

01

Product Catalog & Online Purchases

"Where can I purchase smoked products online and what products are currently available?"

Solution: A visually detailed "Products" page featuring clear imagery, descriptive flavor profiles, prices, and an intuitive "Add to Cart" or direct ordering dynamic. Users can instantly filter by category (Sausages, Beef, Chicken, Pork) and proceed to a secure checkout.

02

Wholesale & B2B Inquiries

"How can I contact the business to place a wholesale order for my restaurant or store?"

Solution: A dedicated "Wholesale Section" and interactive form allowing business owners to input their restaurant name, contact details, quantity needs, and instantly request specialized pricing catalogs or order custom samples.

03

Brand Differentiation & Storytelling

"What makes Humo y Sabor different from other smoked food businesses?"

Solution: An "About Us" section detailing the traditional artisanal processes, the specific hardwoods used for smoking, the family heritage, and the authentic Colombian flavors that distinguish these premium recipes from mass-produced products.

04

Location & Contact Details

"Where is the business located and how can I contact the owners?"

Solution: A modern contact block within the website incorporating active maps, social media links, email addresses, and a responsive phone button for direct customer service inquiries.

Visual Identity

Color Scheme

Our color strategy relies on rich, deep shades paired with warm tones to convey comfort, wood-fired smoke, and premium gourmet quality. Subtle details integrate the official colors of Colombia.

Primary Palette

Premium Black #121212
Smoked Wood Brown #5C2E1F
Premium Gold #D4A017
Warm Cream #F9EAC8
Light Cream #FDF8ED

Accent Palette (Colombian Heritage)

Colombian Red #CA0F25
Colombian Blue #003893
Colombian Yellow #FCD116

Application & Implementation

  • Backgrounds: Premium Black (#121212) serves as the primary base for a luxurious visual appeal. Light Cream (#FDF8ED) and Warm Cream (#F9EAC8) are utilized in specific card backdrops or section layers to keep readability high.
  • Headings: Premium Gold (#D4A017) and Light Cream (#FDF8ED) establish beautiful editorial typographic hierarchy.
  • Accent Elements & Details: Colombian Red (#CA0F25), Blue (#003893), and Yellow (#FCD116) will highlight tiny decorative details, custom divider borders, and regional tags signaling authentic Colombian recipes.
  • Buttons & Call-to-Actions: Premium Gold (#D4A017) with Smoked Wood Brown (#5C2E1F) details will highlight crucial conversion prompts.
Typography Strategy

Typography

Heading Font: Cormorant Garamond

Serif | Elegant, Traditional, Editorial

H1 Example: Humo y Sabor H2 Example: El Arte del Ahumado

Usage: Used for all primary page titles, structural section headings, and product names. The elegant, classic serif style mirrors artisanal, gourmet traditions and establishes immediate visual authority.

Body Text Font: Inter

Sans-Serif | Modern, Highly Readable, Clean

Paragraph Example: Nuestro proceso artesanal garantiza que cada pieza de carne conserve sus jugos y adquiera un aroma ahumado incomparable.

Usage: Applied to paragraph body copy, utility menus, specifications, metadata labels, and primary action buttons. Inter ensures optimal digital legibility on both mobile screens and high-resolution desktops.
