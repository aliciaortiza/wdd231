About Us

My name is Alicia Ortiz, and I am passionate about exploring the world and connecting with people. Traveling allows me to discover new cultures, traditions, and cuisines, which constantly inspires me to grow both personally and professionally. I thrive in communities where I feel safe and supported, and I love building healthy relationships that encourage learning and personal development—especially alongside other amazing women. Currently, I am pursuing a degree in Systems Engineering and Software Development, combining my love for technology with my curiosity for problem-solving and innovation. Born and raised in Guadalupe, San José, Costa Rica, I carry with me the warmth of my culture while embracing opportunities to expand my knowledge and experiences globally.
